# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central index and quick overview of OctoAcme's project management processes. These documents provide shared guidance for initiating, planning, delivering, releasing, and improving cross-functional work.

## Process summary

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project lifecycle. During initiation, teams validate the problem, define measurable outcomes and success criteria, identify stakeholders, estimate initial resource needs, and confirm a go/no-go decision. Approved initiatives move into planning, where the team creates a prioritized backlog, writes acceptance criteria, estimates scope, documents the Definition of Done, identifies dependencies and risks, and maps releases and milestones.

During execution, teams track work on a project board, use small pull requests linked to issues, and maintain regular standups, delivery syncs, demos, and reviews. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, status reporting, and stakeholder communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers implement and test the work; QA/testing validates quality and acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and blockers are recorded in shared artifacts and escalated from the team to the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor when necessary.

Quality is built into delivery through unit, integration, and end-to-end testing as appropriate, automated CI tests and linting, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and smoke tests for critical flows. Before release, acceptance criteria must be complete, checks must pass, release notes and rollback or mitigation plans must be ready, and deployment verification must be performed. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, teams hold retrospectives, assign and track improvement actions, measure their impact, and use the results to continuously improve how work is delivered.
